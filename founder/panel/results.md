## What would flip a no

- A free trial or a month-to-month option with no monthly plan required, plus proof that the live video and SOS alert really work from a wandering-prone wearer's device without a long setup.
- If she could understand it and agree to it, the camera only switched on when she pressed the SOS button, and a doctor or dementia specialist told me it helps her and does not upset her.
- Independent reviews or a parent I know who has used it for a few months with real reliability, plus a clear return policy so I'm not locked in.
- A GPS-only mode with no camera, with wandering alerts and a clear rule that video is never on unless a caregiver is responding to an SOS.
- A free or refundable trial where I can test a live video call with Mom from two hours away, with a clear answer on what happens when the signal is weak.
- A clear written policy showing video is only viewed when there is a real alert, plus a setup I could finish in under ten minutes with no tech help.
- Honest reviews from other parents of wandering children showing the GPS and SOS working in a real incident, plus a trial period with a full refund if the device does not do what it says.
- Independent reviews from caregivers who have used it for months, showing the battery lasts a full day with live video on and the device survives a dementia patient pulling at it.
- A real owner in a caregiver group telling me it connected reliably during a wandering episode, or a free or refundable trial I can run on mum before I commit.
- Other caregivers in my Facebook groups and Reddit threads reporting that it works reliably for a wanderer, plus a month-to-month option where I can cancel the plan at any time.
- Caregiver groups and independent reviews showing it has actually found a wandering loved one quickly, plus a clear way to switch the camera off that keeps the GPS and SOS working.
- Dementia caregivers in the support groups posting that it stays on, the GPS holds up in her neighbourhood, and there is a real return policy if it doesn't work.
