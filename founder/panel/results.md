## What would flip a no

- A parent in the group who has used it through a real wandering episode and tells me it connected within a minute and the wearer kept it on for weeks.
- If she starts wandering or getting lost on her own, and the 30-day trial shows she will actually wear it without a fight, I would switch from the AirTag to this.
- Independent reviews from other autism or dementia caregivers saying it connected on a real bad day, plus a clear answer on who stores the footage and location data.
- A version with GPS and the SOS button but no camera, or a written, independent check that the camera can never be turned on without her or the family agreeing to it.
- A test video connection that works reliably from two hours away, during a real walk, with a harness she cannot take off, and a clear answer on what happens to my existing tracker.
- A GPS-and-SOS version with no camera, or a camera that only turns on when he presses the button and the family agrees to it in writing, plus a trial where I can see it work in our house first.
- A family I know who has the same kind of child has used it on a real wander and it connected, with the founder or an independent reviewer confirming that it works.
- Honest, verifiable reviews from caregivers in the Facebook groups I read, showing the battery lasts a full day with live video and the SOS connects reliably.
- Three or four families in my support groups posting honest reviews after a few months of use, saying it connected every time and their kid kept it on.
- Independent reviews from parents of wandering kids in the Facebook and Reddit groups saying it connected when it mattered and the camera stayed off as promised, plus a clear written statement on who can view the footage.
- Reviews from other families with early-stage dementia in the Alzheimer's support groups I read, saying the device stayed on for weeks and the SOS actually connected when it was needed.
- A GPS-and-SOS version with no camera at all, or a camera that only the family can switch on with the wearer told in advance how it works, at a price that beats an AirTag plus a watch.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
