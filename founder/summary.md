# Summary

**Verdict: Not yet.** Three of four checks pass. The one that fails is **demand: 4 of 20 simulated buyers buy (20%) against a 25% bar.** The fix isn't a lower price. It's **trust**: 9 of the 16 who passed said some version of "I've never heard of TODO". That gets fixed by the reservation test and the family pilot (`/founder-launch`), then re-testing the offer with real reviews (`/founder-offer`). A smaller group of dementia caregivers rejects any camera; a camera-free GPS + SOS version is worth testing (`/founder-offer`, `/founder-pricing`).

**What it is:** TODO is a wearable with an app that lets a family member find, hear and (during an SOS) see a loved one who may wander or get lost. It's for US parents of children with autism or intellectual disabilities first, and adult children of parents with dementia second. $249 device + $24.99/month.

**The numbers (all costs estimated, no quotes yet):** each new customer leaves **$130.88 (27%)** after their own costs, break-even is about **1 new customer a day**, and year 1 makes **$19,511** operating profit at plan. The biggest assumption is ad cost per customer ($60). At $180, year 1 loses money.

**Board and panel agree:**
- the need is real and emotional;
- trust and proof decide the sale;
- wearers taking it off and battery life are the product risks;
- it must focus on one group first.

**They disagree:** the board's strongest version is special-needs children only; the panel's dementia caregivers bought at a similar rate but raised the strongest privacy objections.

**Biggest risk:** the device fails at the one moment it matters (signal, battery, or the wearer took it off). **Plan:** test samples from 3–4 factories for real battery life and SOS reliability, run a 3-week pilot with 6–10 families, and cap live video so data costs stay under the plan price.

**What you need to start:** about **$12,807 on paper and realistically $18–20k** (you have $10k), plus a privacy/COPPA lawyer review and a device that already has a valid FCC ID. First test: a landing page with a $25 refundable reservation, 16 Nov to 13 Dec 2026. Success = 25 or more reservations at 2% or more conversion.

**This week:** send the spec and the FCC/battery/LTE/SDK questions to 6–8 factories and order samples from the best 3–4, and get the reservation landing page live by Sunday 15 Nov.
