# Marketing: TODO Safe Return Kit (US)

Sources: `panel/results.md` (re-test 4/20 buy), `panel-v1/results.md`, `competitors.md`, `offer.md`, `pricing.md`, `cfo.md`. Panel quotes are research only. **Never use them in ads.**

## 1. Who first
The buyers came from **crisis buyers** (just had a wandering scare or a police call) and **families already paying for a tracker** once the refund and battery were added. Both main segments bought at similar rates. The **first audience is parents of children with autism who wander**: they're easier to reach (dense online and school communities), and the "values" objection came almost entirely from dementia caregivers. Dementia families are second.

## 2. Positioning
1. *For parents of a child who wanders, who lie awake wondering "where is he right now?", TODO is the safety wearable that lets you see, hear and find them the moment something is wrong, unlike trackers that only show a dot on a map.*
2. *For families caring for someone who might get lost, TODO is the only wearable that adds a live view to GPS, voice and SOS, and the camera stays off until they need you.*
3. *For AngelSense families who want more, TODO gives you eyes, not just ears, for about half the monthly price.*

**Recommended: #1.** The buyers' reasons were the scare and finding him fast ("Live video and GPS on him would let me find him fast"). #2's privacy line goes into every ad as the answer to the values objection. #3 is a retargeting ad for tracker owners only.

## 3. Channels
| channel | why it fits | rough cost | worked if… |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Parent communities** (autism-parent Facebook groups, r/Autism_Parenting, local special-needs parent groups) | Where "researches for weeks" buyers decide; trust comes from peers | $0 + founder time; follow each group's self-promo rules, ask admins first | ≥30 waitlist sign-ups from groups by launch |
| **Partners**: special-needs schools, ABA/OT therapists, local autism societies, sheriff Project Lifesaver coordinators | Trusted voices answer "never heard of TODO" | Free demo kits (2–3 kits ≈ $465 at cost) | ≥3 partners agree to share a flyer or pilot |
| **Meta ads** (Facebook/Instagram) targeted at parent/caregiver interests, retargeting site visitors | Cheapest way to test ad cost per customer, the CFO's biggest unknown | $600 of the $1,000 launch budget | Cost per $25 deposit ≤ $60 |
| **Founder content** (short videos: real-walk tests, battery tests, setup in 10 minutes) | Proves reliability, the #1 flip condition | $0 | Videos get saves/shares; comments ask "where can I get it" |

**Not doing yet:** Google search ads (people searching "autism tracker" find AngelSense; costly to compete), TikTok influencers (trust risk with kids' safety; later, with disclosed partnerships), retail/Amazon (margin and returns), hospitals/nursing homes (board said later).

## 5. Ten hooks
Each one answers a panel objection.
1. **Video, 3s:** "Where is he right now? Now you can see."
2. **Ad headline:** "The camera stays off until they press SOS." *(surveillance worry)*
3. **Video:** "I tried to pull this off my own chest. Here's the harness." *(they'll pull it off)*
4. **Ad:** "Spare battery in the box. Swap it in 10 seconds." *(battery)*
5. **Ad:** "Use it on a real walk for 30 days, or get every dollar back." *(will it connect / trust)*
6. **Ad:** "No contract. Cancel in the app." *(another monthly fee / lock-in)*
7. **Video:** "An AirTag shows a dot. This shows you what they see." *(AirTag does most of it)*
8. **Retarget:** "Set up together on a 10-minute video call." *(low-tech)*
9. **Ad, tracker owners:** "Eyes, not just ears, for about half the monthly price." *(already have AngelSense)* Check that the comparison is accurate and current before running it.
10. **Video:** "What happens the moment they press SOS." *(panic / will it work)*

## 6. Budget and numbers
- **Budget (from the $1,000 launch line):** Meta ads $600 · demo kits for partners are already in inventory (2–3 kits) · printed flyers for partners $100 · landing-page tools $100 · reserve $200.
- **The most you can pay to win a customer:** each customer leaves $190.88 *before* ads ($130.88 contribution + the $60 ad estimate already in it) × 1.0 purchase per buyer (panel) = **$190 to break even on the first year**. **Target ≤ $95** (half), so the business keeps a real margin. The CFO shows year 1 losing money at $180.
- **Watch weekly:**
  1. **Ad cost per $25 deposit:** change the hook or audience if > $60. Stop if > $95 after $300 spent.
  2. **Landing-page deposit rate:** fix the page or offer if < 2% of visitors.
  3. **Founding families wearing it daily at day 14:** below 70%, pause ads and fix the product. Word of mouth from those families decides the rest.

*Ads must not claim medical benefits or guaranteed safety. "Helps you find them faster" is fine; "keeps them safe" is a promise you can't guarantee. Follow FTC endorsement rules for any partner or creator content. Not legal advice.*
