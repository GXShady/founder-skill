# Pricing: TODO

All cost inputs are **estimates** (see `numbers.json`). Price answers come from 20 simulated buyers. Use them to pick what to test, not as proof.

## 1. The price: **$249 device + $24.99/month (or $19.99/month paid yearly)**

- **Buyers:** the acceptable range is $91–$280 (`pricing-curve.md`). The median buyer starts to find it expensive at $249 and too expensive at $320. $249 is inside the range but at the top, so it needs a reason to believe (trial, proof).
- **Competitors:** devices run ~$100–$230. Special-needs and senior plans run $30–$65/month (AngelSense $44.99–$64.99; Medical Guardian $39.95–$46.95; Bay Alarm $29.95–$39.95). Kids' watches run $10–$18/month. At $24.99 TODO costs **less per month than every special-needs competitor** while being the only one with live video.
- **Margin (CFO tool, estimated costs):**

| device price | contribution per device | break-even | year 1 operating profit (device only) |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $199 | -$35.46 (loses money) | never | -$13,674 |
| **$249** | **$14.54 (6%)** | **2/day** | **-$3,699** |
| $299 | $64.54 (22%) | 1/day | +$6,276 |

  **The device alone barely breaks even at $249, because the estimated $60 of ads per sale eats the margin. The monthly plan is where the money is.** At ~$10/month of data and cloud cost per user (estimate) and 5% of subscribers cancelling each month, year 1 subscription contribution is about **$8,000 at $19.99** or **$12,000 at $24.99** on the same ramp. Together that turns year 1 positive. `/founder-cfo` models it properly.
- **Never go below $199 on the device.** At that price each sale loses money before fixed costs.

## 2. The ladder
| tier | what | price | why step up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SOS** | GPS + SOS + two-way voice; camera turns on only during an SOS | $24.99/mo (or $19.99/mo yearly) | Answers the privacy/dignity objection; matches AngelSense on features at about half the price |
| **Live** | Everything in SOS + live video on demand, video history | $34.99/mo | For crisis families who want to see, not just hear |
| **Family** | Live + up to 4 caregivers + priority support | $44.99/mo | Long-distance siblings sharing care for a parent |

The device price is the same for every tier. Upgrading is a switch in the app, which also tests what buyers really value.

## 3. Opening offer (launch only, ends 60 days after launch)
- **30-day full-refund trial** (device and plan). This is the #1 flip condition in the panel. It is not a discount, so it doesn't teach people to wait for sales.
- **First 100 families:** first month free plus a founding-family badge and a direct line to the founder for feedback (this also produces the reviews the panel asked for).
- No "was $X" prices.
