## 3. The stack
- **Core:** "See, hear and find your loved one the moment something is wrong." Device + app. GPS always on, two-way voice, one-press SOS, and live video **only during an SOS or a visible check-in**. Month-to-month from $24.99, no contract.
- **Bonus 1: Wear-It-Their-Way Kit.** Clip, lanyard, armband and a locking harness for wearers who pull things off.
- **Bonus 2: Spare Battery + low-battery alert.** Swap in 10 seconds, and you get a warning before it dies.
- **Bonus 3: 10-minute setup call + SOS card.** Set up together on video, with the steps on a fridge card.
- **Guarantee: "Test It on a Real Walk."** Use it for 30 days. If it doesn't connect when you need it, or they won't wear it, send it back for a full refund of device and plan.
- **True urgency:** the founding batch is 50 units (first inventory), and the founding-family perks end when it's gone or 60 days after launch.
- **Name:** **TODO Safe Return Kit**

**Cost of the stack (estimate):** +$4 harness, +$8 spare battery, setup calls cost founder time. That's about **+$12 per device**. The guarantee is modelled at 10% of buyers returning; the 6% reserve already in `numbers.json` covers most of that, and `/founder-cfo` re-runs it.

## 5. Re-test
Same customer, same seed (732778), new pitch. Results below.

**Result: 2 buy (10%) → 4 buy (20%)**, same 20 simulated buyers, same seed. Full results: `panel/results.md` (v1 in `panel-v1/`).
- **Trust** passes: 11 → 9. The refund helps, but "I've never heard of TODO" survives every offer. **Only real families using it fixes this**, which means a pilot plus published reviews (`/founder-launch`).
- **Values** passes: 3 → 4. For some dementia caregivers, *any* camera is a no, even SOS-only. They ask for a **GPS + SOS version with no camera**. Consider a cheaper camera-free SKU, or treat these buyers as not TODO's market.
- **Price** passes: 4 → 3. The objection now is "a second monthly bill on top of AngelSense", so the switch credit matters.
- New buyers came from the "already uses a tracker" and "crisis" groups, which suggests the refund, spare battery and harness answered their concerns.
- One buyer said the name "TODO" reads as unfinished (P017). Flag for `/founder-brand`.
