# Brand: TODO Safe Return Kit

Built on positioning #1 in `marketing.md`: *see, hear and find them the moment something is wrong.* The first audience is parents of a child who wanders.

## 1. The name
"TODO" is the working name. One simulated buyer said it "reads as unfinished" (P017). In English, "to-do" also reads as a chore list, and it is hard to search for or trademark as a plain word. **Recommendation: keep TODO only if it means something to you; otherwise rename before you buy packaging.**

| # | name | style | what it says | out loud / on an icon | risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | TODO | current | nothing yet; sounds like "to-do" | short, clean | reads unfinished; generic word, hard to search or own |
| 2 | **Kinlens** | invented | family (kin) + seeing (lens) | "KIN-lens", clear; K mark works at 32px | lens = camera can feel like surveillance |
| 3 | **Homeward** | evocative | getting them back home | warm, easy to spell | common word; likely crowded in trademarks |
| 4 | **Beacon & Kin** | evocative | a light that finds family | friendly | long for an icon |
| 5 | Wanderwell | evocative | wandering made safe | soft | could suggest wandering is fine |
| 6 | SafeReturn | descriptive | exactly the job | clear | generic; similar to existing "safe return" programs |
| 7 | **Tether** | evocative | a gentle line between you | one syllable, strong | generic word; likely taken in tech |
| 8 | Findly | invented | finding | modern | sounds like many apps |
| 9 | NearMe | descriptive | they're close | simple | far too generic |
| 10 | Lumo | invented | light in the dark | cute, short | likely taken; doesn't say what it does |

**Shortlist: Kinlens, Homeward, Tether** (with TODO kept as the founder's option).

### Checks you must run (I could only plan them; this session's network blocks domain lookups)
- **Trademark (US):** search [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) for each name in **Class 9** (cameras, GPS devices, software apps) and **Class 45** (personal safety/monitoring services). Also check **Class 38** (telecom/streaming).
- **Domain:** check `<name>.com` at any registrar. If it's taken, try `get<name>.com`, `<name>safety.com`, `<name>.care`.
- **Handles:** Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, plus a Facebook page (groups are where your buyers are).
- **Confusion:** none of the shortlist is close to AngelSense, Amber Alert GPS, Theora, Jiobit, TickTalk, Bark or Gabb. Avoid any name with "Angel" or "Amber".

These are public searches only. A trademark lawyer should confirm before you register, and nothing is yours until you file.

## 2. Voice and promise
- **Promise:** *When something is wrong, you'll know where they are, see what's happening, and be able to talk to them, in seconds.*
- **Tagline options:** "See them. Hear them. Find them." · "Your line to them, when it matters." · "Safety that respects them."
- **Voice:**
  - **Calm:** *do* write like a steady friend in a crisis. *Don't* use fear ("Every second counts!", sirens, red alerts).
  - **Honest:** *do* say what it can't do (dead zones, battery limits). *Don't* promise "keeps them safe" or "never lose them".
  - **Respectful:** *do* talk about the wearer as a person with dignity. *Don't* use "monitor", "track your patient" or "surveillance".
- **Before → after** (pitch line):
  - Before: "A small camera your child or parent wears. GPS is always on."
  - After: "They wear it. You know where they are. The moment they press the button, you can see, hear and talk to them."

## 3. The look (brief)
| colour | hex | job | text contrast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Night Navy | `#1F3A5F` | main brand colour; calm, trustworthy | white on navy **11.5:1** ✔ AA/AAA |
| Warm Cream | `#FFF8EE` | backgrounds, packaging | navy on cream **10.9:1** ✔ |
| Beacon Amber | `#F2A33A` | the SOS button, highlights (not alarm-red) | navy on amber **5.5:1** ✔ AA; *don't* put white text on amber |
| Deep Ember (for text-on-colour buttons) | `#B23A0B` | buttons with white text | white on ember **6.0:1** ✔ AA |

- **Type:** display **Fraunces** (soft, human serif) and text **Inter**. Both are free under the SIL Open Font License from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/).
- **Logo brief:** a simple mark that reads at 32px. Ideas: a soft circle/beacon, or the name's initial as a gentle "line home". It must work on the device itself (one colour, embossed), the app icon, the box and a flyer. **Avoid:** eyes, camera lenses, crosshairs, police or medical crosses, anything that looks like surveillance or a hospital device (buyers said dads won't wear "medical-looking" things). I can generate concepts if an image tool is connected; anything generated is a concept, not a finished logo.
- **The device:** offer navy, cream and one "fun" colour for kids. Nothing that looks like a police body cam.

## 4. The first five touchpoints
1. **Homepage hero:** "See them. Hear them. Find them." A real (consented) family photo, the 3-step "they wear it, they press it, you see and talk", then "30-day real-walk refund".
2. **Packaging:** cream box, navy type. Inside the lid: "Set up together: book your 10-minute call →" with a QR code, and the "first 60 seconds of an SOS" card on top.
3. **Order confirmation:** "Your kit ships in X days. Here's what to expect, and your direct line to [founder]. If anything worries you, reply to this email; a real person reads it."
4. **First social post:** founder on camera: "Why I'm building this, and why I need 10 parents to try to break it."
5. **Reply to the first complaint:** "I'm sorry, that's exactly what we can't let happen. Here's what I'm doing today: [fix / replacement / refund]. Can I call you?" Public reply short, details in private, always within 24h.

*Not legal advice on trademarks.*
