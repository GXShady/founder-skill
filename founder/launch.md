# Launch plan: TODO Safe Return Kit

Today: **Fri 6 Nov 2026**. Target public launch: **Tue 16 Mar 2027**. This is the earliest realistic date, given no prototype yet, sample testing, a family pilot, the legal review and a 3–6 week factory lead time (`ops.md`).

## 1. Test before you spend (start now, before buying 50 kits)
**The test:** a landing page with the real offer ($249 + $24.99/mo, 30-day refund) and a **$25 fully refundable reservation**, clearly labelled "Founding batch, expected to ship March 2027". Traffic: parent groups (with admin OK), 2–3 partners, and $300 of Meta ads.
- **Runs:** Mon 16 Nov → Sun 13 Dec 2026 (4 weeks)
- **Success line (write it down before starting):** **≥ 25 paid reservations** and **≥ 2% of visitors reserving**, at **≤ $60 ad cost per reservation**.
- **Compare with the panel:** the panel bought at 20% (simulated, an upper bound). A real 2% page conversion is normal for a new brand; below 1% means the message or offer is wrong.
  - **Hit it:** go ahead with samples and the pilot.
  - **Under 10 reservations:** stop and go back to `/founder-offer` or `/founder-pricing` (test the camera-free GPS + SOS version, or a lower device price).
- **Rules:** take reservations only for something you intend to ship, give a realistic ship date, refund on request, and email everyone if the date slips (US mail-order rules require shipping within the stated time or offering a refund).

**Test 2: the family pilot (Jan 2027).** Give 6–10 pilot families (from the reservation list, mixed autism and dementia) a sample for 3 weeks, free or at cost, in exchange for honest feedback. **Success line:** ≥ 70% still wearing it daily at day 21, every test SOS connects, battery lasts a waking day with the spare. Ask permission to share their real feedback (in their own words, disclosed). This is what answers "never heard of TODO".
